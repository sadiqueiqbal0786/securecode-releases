SecureCode Auditor — how to open it the first time
==================================================

1. Drag "SecureCode Auditor" onto the Applications folder.

2. Open it from Applications. macOS says it cannot verify the app
   ("Apple could not verify ... is free of malware"). Click Done.
   This happens because the app is not yet notarised by Apple — it is
   signed, and the download has not been changed.

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
   "SecureCode Auditor was blocked", and click "Open Anyway".
   Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" again.

That is only needed once. After that it opens like any other app.

Prefer Terminal? This does the same (after step 1):

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/SecureCode Auditor.app"

Check your download (optional): in Terminal run
    shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/SecureCode-Auditor-*.dmg
and compare it with the checksum published next to the download.

Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), and Docker Desktop,
OrbStack, Colima or Podman — the app's first-run guide helps with that.
