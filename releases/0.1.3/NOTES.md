SecureCode Auditor 0.1.3 for Apple Silicon Macs.

**Clean-up is now a walk-through.** It used to be one screen with every control on it, which asked you to understand the whole procedure before the first click. Now it is one decision per step, with a row of steps along the top showing where you are.

- **Cleaning a copy on your Mac** is three steps: look, see exactly what would be deleted, done. The first step only looks — nothing changes until the step that says it will.
- **Rebuilding a repository** is five: check who can push (skippable), say who is making the change, decide any files the app will not guess about, read and approve the exact change, then ask GitHub to purge the old history.
- Finishing a step carries you to the next one; Back always works; and a step whose own answer is what you were waiting for — the GitHub check — keeps you there to read it.

Everything the clean-up could do before, it still does, with the same approvals and the same safety checks.

**Install:** download `SecureCode-Auditor-0.1.3-apple-silicon.dmg`, drag the app to Applications, and follow *How to open* (one-time "Open Anyway" in Privacy & Security — the app is signed but not yet notarised).

**Needs:** Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13+, and Docker Desktop, OrbStack, Colima or Podman.
